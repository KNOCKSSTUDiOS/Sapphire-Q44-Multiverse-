# Privacy Policy — KNOCKS STUDiOS LLC
**hollywoodimaging.studio** | Effective: January 1, 2025

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account data**: name, email, billing address
- **Payment data**: processed securely via Stripe — we never store card numbers
- **Usage data**: pages visited, features used, session duration
- **Device data**: IP address, browser type, OS, device identifiers

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- Deliver and improve our services
- Process subscription payments
- Send transactional and marketing emails (opt-out available)
- Detect and prevent fraud and abuse
- Comply with legal obligations

## 3. Data Sharing
We do NOT sell your personal data. We share data only with:
- **Stripe** (payment processing)
- **Supabase** (database and authentication)
- **Cloudflare** (CDN and security)
- Law enforcement when legally required

## 4. Data Retention
Account data is retained while your account is active and for 90 days after deletion. Payment records are retained for 7 years per tax law requirements.

## 5. Your Rights (CCPA / GDPR)
- Access, correct, or delete your personal data
- Opt out of marketing communications
- Data portability upon request
- Contact: privacy@hollywoodimaging.studio

## 6. Cookies
We use essential cookies for authentication and analytics cookies (opt-out available). We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

## 7. Security
All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. We conduct regular security audits. Breach notifications will be sent within 72 hours.

## 8. Children
This platform is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
